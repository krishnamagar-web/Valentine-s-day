My Promise To You 🤞

You promised me to stay, I promise to never leave.

I promise tumhare sath koi khada ho ya na ho, main tumhare sath hamesha khada rahunga.

I promise ki tumhare sukh mein na sahi, lekin tumhare dukh mein hamesha tumhare sath rahunga.

I promise tumhe sunne wala koi nahi hoga, toh main tumhari baatein hamesha sununga.

Lekin ye promise nahi kar sakta ki hum kabhi ladai nahi karenge 😅 choti-moti toh ladai hogi… pyaar wali 🤭💖
